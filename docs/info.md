## External hardware

- RC low-pass filter on `pwm_out` to recover analog audio.
- I2S DAC (e.g. PCM5102-style) on `i2s_bclk` / `i2s_lrclk` / `i2s_sdata`.
- S/PDIF receiver or transformer/coax adapter on `spdif_out`.
- 68k bus (address/data/control lines) per the pinout below, generated by
  the retrocomputer's CPU board; a small external AND gate combines this
  chip's `dtack_n` with any other bus peripherals' — see
  [docs/design/68k-bus-interface.md §3.1](design/68k-bus-interface.md#31-dtack-self-generated-plain-push-pull-output).
